How Home Loan Repayments Work in South Africa

South African banks (FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, and Capitec/SA Home Loans) typically price home loans relative to the Prime Lending Rate over a standard 20-year repayment term. Putting down a 10% to 20% deposit lowers your loan-to-value ratio and can help you negotiate a lower interest rate below prime.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SARS Transfer Duty threshold?
Properties purchased for R1,210,000 or less are completely exempt from SARS Transfer Duty (R0). Above R1,210,000, transfer duty is calculated on a progressive sliding scale starting at 3% on the portion above R1,210,000. Note that attorney bond registration and transfer fees still apply separately.

How much household income do I need to qualify for a bond?
Most South African banks require your monthly bond instalment to be no more than 30% of your gross monthly household income, subject to your credit score and monthly expenses under the National Credit Act.

How much can paying extra save on a South African home loan?
Even paying an extra R1,000 per month on a R1.35 million bond can cut years off your loan term and save hundreds of thousands of Rands in interest.
