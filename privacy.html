Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Local Browser File Processing

LOGISOPH Tools is designed to protect your privacy. All image, PDF, text, and calculator tools process data directly inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not upload, store, view, or transmit your files to any external server.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user accounts or personal registration to use our tools. Standard technical logs (such as browser type, referring pages, and approximate region) may be collected automatically by our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages) to maintain security and performance.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

To keep LOGISOPH Tools free for all users, we may display advertisements through third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

4. POPIA & International Privacy Compliance

We respect user privacy rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) as well as international data protection standards. Because your files never leave your browser, we do not hold copies of your uploaded documents.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
