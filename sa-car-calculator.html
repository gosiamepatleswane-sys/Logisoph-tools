How Balloon Payments Work in South Africa

A balloon payment (also called a residual value) is a lump-sum percentage of the vehicle's purchase price set aside until the very end of your finance agreement. While it lowers your monthly instalments during the loan term, you still pay interest on the balloon amount throughout the contract—and you must either pay that lump sum in cash, refinance it, or trade in the car when the final month arrives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a balloon payment a good idea?
A balloon payment makes monthly cash flow easier in the short term, but it increases your total interest paid and creates a large lump-sum debt at the end. If you plan to keep the car for more than 5 years, choosing 0% balloon with a 10%–20% deposit saves the most money.

What extra costs should I budget for when buying a car in SA?
Beyond your monthly instalment, South African banks require comprehensive car insurance on financed vehicles, plus a once-off initiation fee (around R1,207) and a monthly admin fee (around R69).
