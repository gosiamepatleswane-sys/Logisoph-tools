Frequently Asked Questions

Does Life Orientation (LO) count toward my APS score?
Most South African universities—including the University of Pretoria (UP), University of Johannesburg (UJ), UNISA, NWU, and TUT—exclude Life Orientation when calculating your APS out of 42 (though you must still pass LO). Wits University awards up to 4 points for LO (4 points for 80%+, 3 for 70–79%, 2 for 60–69%, 1 for 50–59%).

What APS do I need for a Bachelor's Degree, Diploma, or Higher Certificate?
Entry requirements vary by university and faculty, but typically a Higher Certificate starts around 15–18 APS, a Diploma starts around 19–24 APS, and a Bachelor's Degree generally requires 24 to 36+ APS alongside specific subject minimums in English and Mathematics.

Disclaimer: Always verify exact faculty admission requirements and Mathematical Literacy rules directly in your chosen university's official prospectus.
