How Loan Amortization Works

With a fixed-rate amortized loan, your monthly payment stays the same throughout the entire loan term. In the early months, a larger portion of each payment goes toward interest; as the balance drops, more of each payment reduces your principal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do extra monthly payments save money?
Any extra amount you pay above your required monthly instalment goes directly toward reducing your principal balance, which lowers the interest charged in every remaining month and shortens your payoff timeline.

Can I use this for both personal loans and mortgages?
Yes. Simply enter your loan amount (for example, $25,000 over 5 years for a car or personal loan, or $300,000 over 30 years for a mortgage) and switch the currency symbol to match your country.
