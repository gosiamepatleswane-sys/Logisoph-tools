How to Use the South African VAT Calculator

  1. Enter your Rand (ZAR) figure in the Amount field.
  2. Select Add 15% VAT if your price excludes VAT, or Extract 15% VAT if your total already includes VAT.
  3. Copy the exact VAT-exclusive amount, VAT portion, and VAT-inclusive invoice total from the breakdown box.
