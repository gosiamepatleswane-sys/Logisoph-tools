Frequently Asked Questions (SARS 2026/2027)

What is the tax-free threshold for 2026/2027?
If you are under 65, you pay zero income tax if your annual taxable income is below R99,000 (R8,250/month). For ages 65–74 the threshold is R153,250, and for 75 and older it is R171,300.

How is UIF calculated in South Africa?
Employees contribute 1% of gross monthly remuneration to the Unemployment Insurance Fund (UIF), capped at the monthly earnings ceiling of R17,712. That means the maximum employee UIF deduction is R177.12 per month.

How do medical aid tax credits work?
For 2026/2027, SARS grants a monthly Medical Scheme Fees Tax Credit of R376 each for the main member and first dependant, and R254 for every additional dependant.
