Frequently Asked Questions

How much notice do I need to give when resigning in South Africa?
Under South Africa's Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA), minimum statutory notice is 1 week if employed for 6 months or less, 2 weeks if employed for more than 6 months up to 1 year, and 4 weeks (or 1 calendar month) if employed for more than 1 year—unless your employment contract specifies a different notice period.
